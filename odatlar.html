Odat 21 kunda shakllanadimi?

“21 kun takrorlasang — odat bo'ladi” degan gapni hamma biladi. Olimlar buni tekshirib ko'rishdi: haqiqiy raqam ancha uzunroq va ancha xilma-xil.

MyHarvardPath · Scientific American asosida · ≈ 3 daqiqa o'qish

“21 kun” afsonasi 1960-yilda chiqqan Psycho-Cybernetics kitobidan boshlangan. Uning muallifi, plastik jarroh Maksvell Molts bemorlari operatsiyadan keyin yangi qiyofasiga taxminan uch haftada ko'nikishini kuzatgan. Hech qanday tajriba o'tkazilmagan — lekin “uch hafta” oson eshitilgani uchun bu raqam butun dunyoga tarqalib ketgan.

Tajriba nima ko'rsatdi?

2009-yilda Fillippa Lalli boshchiligidagi London tadqiqotchilari ko'ngillilardan har kuni bitta yangi ishni bajarishni so'rashdi: tushlikda meva yeyish, bir shisha suv ichish yoki kechki ovqatdan oldin 15 daqiqa yugurish. Keyin bu harakat qachon “o'z-o'zidan”, o'ylamasdan bajariladigan bo'lib qolganini o'lchashdi.

66 kunodat avtomatik bo'lishi uchun o'rtacha vaqt
18–254kun — odamlar orasidagi farq shu qadar katta
9%odam 2023-yilgi yangi yil va'dalarini bajargan

Eng muhim omil — har kuni, izchil takrorlash. Bir kunni o'tkazib yuborish esa jarayonni buzmagan.

Odat turi ham muhim

2023-yilda Kaltex olimi Kolin Kamerer va hamkasblari mashinaviy o'rganish yordamida minglab odamlarning ma'lumotlarini tahlil qilishdi. Qo'l yuvish odati bir necha haftada shakllangan, sport zaliga borish odati esa taxminan yarim yil olgan. Qancha ko'p harakat talab qilsa, shuncha uzoq vaqt kerak.

Muddatni sanamang — takrorni sanang. Progress vaqt cho'zilganda ham davom etadi.

Odatni qanday qurish kerak?

  • Aniq reja tuzing. “Ingliz tilini o'rganaman” emas, “dushanba, chorshanba, juma — 20 daqiqa lug'at”.
  • Mavjud odatga bog'lang. Masalan: nonushtadan keyin darhol 10 ta yangi so'z.
  • Mukofot darhol bo'lsin. Yoqtirgan musiqangizni faqat mashg'ulot paytida tinglang.
  • Muhitni tayyorlang. Kitob stolda, telefon boshqa xonada — kerakli narsa ko'z oldida tursin.
  • Kun o'tkazib yuborsangiz, taslim bo'lmang. Maqsadni vaqtincha kichraytiring, keyin yana oshiring.

FLEX yoki IELTSga tayyorgarlik — bu bir oylik sprint emas. 66 kun deyarli ikki oy degani: bugun boshlagan odatingiz imtihon mavsumiga kelib o'z-o'zidan ishlaydigan bo'ladi.

Manba: Jocelyn Solis-Moreira, “How Long Does It Really Take to Form a Habit?”, Scientific American, 2024; Lally va boshq., European Journal of Social Psychology, 2010. Matn — o'zbek tilidagi erkin qisqacha bayon.

Does a habit really form in 21 days?

Everyone knows the saying “repeat it for 21 days and it becomes a habit”. Scientists put it to the test: the real number is much longer and much more varied.

MyHarvardPath · Based on Scientific American · ≈ 3 min read

The “21 days” myth started with the 1960 book Psycho-Cybernetics. Its author, plastic surgeon Maxwell Maltz, observed that his patients got used to their new appearance after surgery in about three weeks. No experiment was ever run, but because “three weeks” sounds easy, the number spread around the world.

What did the experiment show?

In 2009, London researchers led by Phillippa Lally asked volunteers to do one new thing every day: eat a piece of fruit at lunch, drink a bottle of water, or run for 15 minutes before dinner. Then they measured when the action became automatic, done without thinking.

66 daysaverage time for a habit to become automatic
18–254days: that is how much people differed
9%of people kept their 2023 New Year's resolutions

The most important factor was consistent, daily repetition. Missing a single day did not break the process.

The type of habit matters too

In 2023, Caltech scientist Colin Camerer and colleagues used machine learning to analyse data from thousands of people. A handwashing habit formed within a few weeks, while a gym habit took about half a year. The more effort a behaviour requires, the longer it takes.

Do not count the days, count the repetitions. Progress continues even when it takes longer.

How to build a habit

  • Make a specific plan. Not “I will learn English” but “Monday, Wednesday, Friday: 20 minutes of vocabulary”.
  • Attach it to an existing habit. For example: ten new words right after breakfast.
  • Reward yourself immediately. Listen to your favourite music only while you practise.
  • Prepare your environment. Book on the desk, phone in another room: keep what you need in sight.
  • If you miss a day, do not give up. Shrink the goal for a while, then build back up.

Preparing for FLEX or IELTS is not a one-month sprint. Sixty-six days is almost two months: a habit you start today will be running on its own by exam season.

Source: Jocelyn Solis-Moreira, “How Long Does It Really Take to Form a Habit?”, Scientific American, 2024; Lally et al., European Journal of Social Psychology, 2010. Text: a free summary.
