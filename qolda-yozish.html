Qo'lda yozish yoki noutbuk: qaysi biri yaxshiroq?

“Qo'lda yozilgan konspekt yaxshiroq eslab qolinadi” — buni mashhur tajriba isbotlagandek edi. Keyin olimlar uni takrorlab ko'rishdi.

MyHarvardPath · Psychological Science (2014, 2021) asosida · ≈ 3 daqiqa o'qish

2014-yilda Pam Myuller va Deniel Oppenhaymerning “Ruchka klaviaturadan kuchliroq” nomli maqolasi juda mashhur bo'ldi. Ularning tajribasida noutbukda konspekt yozgan talabalar ma'ruzani so'zma-so'z ko'chirishga moyil bo'lgan, qo'lda yozganlar esa fikrni o'z so'zlari bilan qisqartirgan — va tushunchaga oid savollarga yaxshiroq javob bergan.

Takroriy tajriba

Fanda bitta tajriba yetarli emas. 2021-yilda Xezer Urri boshchiligidagi katta jamoa xuddi shu tajribani aynan takrorladi. Natija: qo'lda yozish va noutbuk o'rtasida tushunchani eslab qolishda farq topilmadi. Ular qo'shimcha tahlilda ham statistik jihatdan sezilarli ta'sir ko'rmadi.

Biroq bir qiziq bog'liqlik qoldi: konspektda so'zlar ko'p bo'lsa, natija yaxshiroq; ma'ruzani so'zma-so'z ko'chirish ko'p bo'lsa — yomonroq. Ya'ni muhimi — vosita emas, qanday yozishingiz.

2014original tajriba: qo'lda yozish yutgan
2021takroriy tajriba: farq topilmadi
So'zma-so'zko'chirish — yomonroq natija bilan bog'liq

Klaviatura ham, ruchka ham — faqat asbob. Fikrlash esa sizning ishingiz.

Qanday konspekt yozish kerak?

  • O'z so'zlaringiz bilan yozing. Ma'ruzachi aytganini emas, tushunganingizni.
  • Savol shaklida yozing. “Fotosintez nima?” — keyin bu savol bilan o'zingizni tekshirasiz.
  • Konspektni qayta ishlang. Darsdan keyin 10 daqiqa: qisqartiring, xulosa yozing.
  • Chalg'ituvchilarni yoping. Noutbukda faqat bitta hujjat ochiq tursin.

Shuningdek, noutbukning haqiqiy xavfi boshqa joyda: unda boshqa oynalar, chatlar va video bor. Konspekt uchun noutbuk ishlatsangiz, internetni o'chirib qo'ying.

Manba: Mueller & Oppenheimer, “The Pen Is Mightier Than the Keyboard”, Psychological Science, 2014; Urry va boshq., “Don’t Ditch the Laptop Just Yet”, Psychological Science, 2021; APS. Matn — o'zbek tilidagi erkin qisqacha bayon.

Handwriting or laptop: which is better for notes?

“Handwritten notes are remembered better”: a famous experiment seemed to prove it. Then scientists tried to repeat it.

MyHarvardPath · Based on Psychological Science (2014, 2021) · ≈ 3 min read

In 2014, a paper by Pam Mueller and Daniel Oppenheimer called “The Pen Is Mightier Than the Keyboard” became famous. In their study, students who typed notes on a laptop tended to copy lectures word for word, while those who wrote by hand summarised ideas in their own words and did better on conceptual questions.

The replication

One experiment is never enough in science. In 2021, a large team led by Heather Urry repeated the same study directly. The result: no difference in conceptual recall between handwriting and typing, and their additional analysis found no statistically significant effect either.

One interesting link remained, though: notes with more words went with better scores, and more word-for-word copying went with worse ones. In other words, what matters is not the tool but how you take notes.

2021replication: no difference found
Verbatimcopying linked to worse results

Keyboard or pen, both are just tools. The thinking is your job.

How to take good notes

  • Use your own words. Write what you understood, not what the speaker said.
  • Write questions. “What is photosynthesis?”: later you can quiz yourself with it.
  • Process your notes. Ten minutes after class: shorten them and write a summary.
  • Close distractions. Keep only one document open on the laptop.

The laptop's real risk lies elsewhere: other windows, chats and videos are one click away. If you take notes on a laptop, switch off the internet.

Source: Mueller & Oppenheimer, “The Pen Is Mightier Than the Keyboard”, Psychological Science, 2014; Urry et al., “Don’t Ditch the Laptop Just Yet”, Psychological Science, 2021; APS. Text: a free summary.
